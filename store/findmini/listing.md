## Full description (EN)

Starweave is a card puzzle roguelike about constellations.

Place star cards on a 4×4 sky. Fill a row or a column and it lights up as a constellation — a Pair, a Flush, a Cascade and more. Close two lines with one card and you weave a cross: the score doubles.

Between nights, visit the Observatory: collect Omens that bend the rules, level up your favourite constellations and shape your deck. Survive eight chapters and their Eclipses.

• Short runs of 10–15 minutes
• A new daily challenge every day
• Challenge a friend: they play the very same deal
• Plays right in Telegram, progress syncs between devices
